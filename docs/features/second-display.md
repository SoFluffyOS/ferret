# Another Mac as a second display

> **TL;DR**
>
> 1. On your Mac, open your other Mac in Ferret and click **Use MacBook Air as a display**.
> 2. On the MacBook Air, click **Allow**.
> 3. Drag a window onto it. That's it.

One screen is never enough. And there's a second Mac on your desk, a perfectly good screen doing nothing.

Ferret turns it into a real extra display for your Mac. Not a mirror: macOS sees one more monitor, so you move windows onto it, arrange it in **System Settings → Displays**, and the other Mac's mouse and keyboard work on it.

## 🖥️ Start it

On the Mac whose desktop you want to extend:

- Open the other Mac in Ferret and click **Use MacBook Air as a display**. It may sit behind **More**.
- The first time, macOS asks to let Ferret record the screen. Ferret only ever captures the extra display it makes, nothing else. Turn Ferret on in **Privacy & Security → Screen Recording**, then quit and open Ferret again.
- Allow Ferret in **Accessibility** too, so clicks on the other Mac land where you point.

Activity says **Asking MacBook Air to be a display…** while you wait.

On the other Mac, Ferret asks **MacBook Pro wants to use this Mac as a display**. Click **Allow**. Its screen turns into your Mac's extra display, full screen and sharp: the display is made the size of that screen, Retina included.

## 🖱️ Use it

- The other Mac's mouse, trackpad and keyboard work on it, shortcuts too. **⌘Q** stays on that Mac, so you never quit its Ferret by accident.
- Move the pointer to the top edge for a small bar with **Full screen** and **Stop**.
- **Esc** leaves full screen. The display keeps going in a window.

## 🔒 You stay in charge

- The other Mac always asks first. Nobody answers in 30 seconds? That's a no.
- Both Macs show it the whole time: **MacBook Air is a display for this Mac** on one, **This Mac is MacBook Pro's display** on the other, each with **Stop**.
- **Stop** on either Mac ends it at once. So does closing the window, losing the connection or quitting Ferret. The extra display goes away, and its windows move back to your Mac's own screen.
- It goes straight between your two Macs, encrypted, over your own network.

## 🧩 Good to know

- Both Macs need this version of Ferret. The one that lends its desktop needs macOS 12.3 or later.
- The extra display tops out at 60 Hz.
- A wired network or a good 5 GHz Wi-Fi makes it feel close to a real monitor.
- 🪟 Windows and 🐧 Linux are coming, both as the screen and as the computer that extends.
