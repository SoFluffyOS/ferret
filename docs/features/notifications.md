# Notifications

> **TL;DR**
>
> 1. On your Android phone, tap **Set up** next to Notifications in **Do more** on Ferret's home screen (or open **Settings → To your computer**), then **Turn on**.
> 2. Turn on Ferret on the Notification access screen, then come back.
> 3. That's it. Every app's new notifications show up on your computer. Reply or dismiss them there.

Your phone buzzes in your bag while you work. You pick it up, unlock it, and it was a message you could have answered from your keyboard.

With Ferret, your phone's notifications show up on your computer. ALL your apps, only new notifications, and never the same one twice.

## 🔔 How it works

- A message arrives on your phone.
- Ferret shows it on your connected computers as a normal notification, with the app's icon, grouped per phone.
- **Reply** right from the notification, or **Dismiss** it.

Dismissing works both ways: dismiss it on your computer and it's gone from your phone, dismiss it on your phone and it's gone from your computer.

The Notifications card on the home screen shows the last few from each phone. **Clear all** empties this computer's list, and your phone keeps its notifications.

## 🧘 No floods

- Only notifications posted **after** your phone connects are sent. Nothing that was already on your phone is replayed.
- The same notification is sent once. An app that re-posts it with the same text doesn't ping you again.
- Ongoing ones (navigation, downloads), music controls, silent ones and group summaries stay on the phone. So do Ferret's own.
- Your computer was asleep? When it reconnects you get **one** summary, "6 notifications while away, from Signal, WhatsApp", not six popups.

## 🔑 Codes stay on your phone

Sign-in, bank and delivery codes don't belong on every computer in the house.

When a notification looks like a one-time code (a 4 to 8 digit number next to a word like "code", "OTP", "verification" or "PIN"), your phone sends the app and title only. Your computer shows **Code hidden. Open it on the phone.**

Want the codes on your computer anyway? On your phone, turn off **Hide one-time codes** in the notification settings.

Android 15 and later also hide these codes from apps like Ferret by themselves.

## 📱 All apps, or just some

Every app comes through by default. Nothing to pick.

Too much? Open **Choose apps** and pick one of three:

- **All apps**: the default.
- **All apps except…**: switch off the few noisy ones. New apps you install come through.
- **Only these apps…**: switch on just the ones you want.

The list has a search box and **Select all** / **Select none**, and the apps that sent you something lately come first.

Find **Choose apps** on the phone in **Settings → To your computer → Notifications**. Or on your computer: open the phone from Home and click **Choose apps**.

Picked apps one by one in an earlier version? Ferret keeps your list as **Only these apps…**. Switch to **All apps** in one tap.

## 🙈 Not while you're using the phone

If your phone's screen is on and unlocked, you already see the notification there. So by default Ferret doesn't mirror it.

Turn **Pause while I'm using the phone** off if you want them on your computer anyway. On your computer, the same switch is on the phone's page: **Hold them back while it's in use**.

## 🔒 Not while the screen is locked

While your computer's screen is locked, or another account is in front, notices say **New notification from Pixel 7** and nothing more. On a computer you share, turn off **Show previews on this computer** for a phone to keep it that way. See [A computer the family shares](../getting-started/shared-computer.md).

## 🔕 Not from this phone

Don't want one phone's notifications on one computer? On that computer, open the phone from Home and turn off **Show its notifications here**. Your other computers keep getting them.

## 🔑 "Android blocked this setting"

On Android 13 and later, Android greys out the Notification access switch for apps installed from a file or from some app stores. Ferret detects it and shows the steps:

1. Tap **Open App info**.
2. Tap **⋮** in the top corner, then **Allow restricted settings**.
3. Come back and open notification access again.

Can't see **Allow restricted settings**? Tap the greyed-out switch once first, then look again.

## 🧭 Limits per platform

| Platform | Sends its notifications | Shows a phone's notifications |
|---|---|---|
| Android | ✅ With notification access | Not yet |
| macOS | No | ✅ Reply and Dismiss |
| Windows | No | ✅ Reply and Dismiss |
| Linux | No | ✅ Reply and Dismiss where your desktop supports actions |
| iOS | No, iOS doesn't let any app read other apps' notifications | While Ferret is open |

Reply works for apps that offer a reply button in their own notification, like most chat apps. Other buttons stay on the phone.

Notification text is never logged and never leaves your network.
