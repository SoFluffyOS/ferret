# Calls

> **TL;DR**
>
> 1. On your Android phone, tap **Set up** next to Calls in **Do more** on Ferret's home screen (or open **Settings → To your computer**), then **Turn on**.
> 2. Allow Phone, Call logs and Contacts when Android asks.
> 3. Your phone rings, your computer shows who it is. **Answer** or **Decline** right there.

Your phone rings in another room. Or in your bag. You dig it out, and it was a call you'd have declined anyway.

With Ferret, your computer shows who's calling the moment your phone rings. Answer, decline, hang up, or start a call, without touching the phone.

## 📞 When your phone rings

- A notification pops up on your computer with the caller's name and two buttons: **Answer** and **Decline**.
- The home screen shows a call card with the name, the number and the contact's photo.
- **Silence** stops the ringtone without declining.

The notification goes away the moment the call is answered or ends, wherever that happened. Nobody picked up? You get one quiet **Missed call** instead.

In Ferret the call sits at the top of **Activity**, with the caller's photo or initials: **Answer**, **Decline** and **Silence** while it rings, a running timer and **Hang up** during the call.

## 🔊 Audio stays on the phone, for now

Ferret answers the call. You talk on the phone, its speaker or its headset.

Sending the call's audio to your computer is planned. It's not in this version, and Ferret says so on the call card instead of pretending.

## ☎️ Call from your computer

Open your phone in Ferret on the computer and pick **Call…**.

- Type a number and hit **Call**. Spaces, dashes and brackets are fine.
- Or pick one from **Recent calls**: the last 50 calls from your phone, with missed ones marked.

Your phone starts the call with its default SIM. Easy, right?

## 🔑 Permissions on the phone

Ferret asks only when you tap **Turn on**, and tells you what each one is for.

| Android permission | What it gives you |
|---|---|
| Phone | See that a call rings, answer, hang up, start calls |
| Call logs | The caller's number, and recent calls |
| Contacts | A name and a photo instead of a number |

Said no to one? Calls still work with what you allowed. **Settings → To your computer** on the phone shows, under Calls, what's on and what's missing, with one button to allow the rest. If Android stopped asking, the button opens the app's settings page instead.

## 🧭 Limits per platform

| Platform | Shares its calls | Shows a phone's calls |
|---|---|---|
| Android | ✅ Answer needs Android 8, hang up and decline need Android 9 | Not yet |
| macOS | No | ✅ |
| Windows | No | ✅ |
| Linux | No | ✅ Answer and Decline on the notification where your desktop supports actions |
| iOS | No, iOS doesn't let apps see or control phone calls | While Ferret is open |

Good to know:

- Android doesn't tell apps when the other side picks up an outgoing call. So an outgoing call shows "Calling" with a timer from the moment it started.
- A second call while you're on one shows up as a new ringing card. Ferret can't tell which of the two is on hold.
- Dual SIM: calls on both SIMs show up. Calls from the computer use your default SIM. If your phone is set to ask every time, Ferret uses the first SIM, since nobody is at the phone to answer the question.
- **Silence** mutes the ringtone until the ringing stops. Android refuses it when it would change Do Not Disturb.
- Emergency numbers open the phone's own dialer. Android doesn't let apps call them directly.

Numbers and names are never logged. They only go to your own paired devices, and never leave your network.
