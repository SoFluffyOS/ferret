# Moving to a new phone

New phone, or the old one was reset? Ferret on it has a new identity, so your computer sees a stranger with your old phone's name. Here's how to move over in a minute, and what comes along.

> TL;DR
> 1. Install Ferret on the new phone and pair it with any ONE of your devices.
> 2. Home asks **Is this your new Pixel 7?** Tap **Replace the old one**.
> 3. That's it. Your other devices follow by themselves.

## 📱 Pair the new phone

Open Ferret on the new phone and pair it like the first time, from **Nearby** or with the QR code (see [Pair your devices](pairing.md)). Pair it with one computer: it joins all your devices from there.

## 🔁 Replace the old one

Right after pairing, Home shows a card:

> **Is this your new Pixel 7?**
> Pixel 7 (3F2A) hasn't been seen since. Replace it, and it goes from all your devices, while this one keeps its name, pin and what it may use.

Tap **Replace the old one**. The new phone takes over:

- 🏷️ the name you gave the old one on this device, such as **My Pixel**
- 📌 its place at the top, if you pinned it
- 🔐 what it may use here (see [What a device can use](../privacy.md))

The old phone is forgotten on every device of yours. Its saved texts on your computer go with it, and the new phone sends its own once you turn **Messages** on.

Really have two phones with the same name? Tap **Keep both**. Ferret tells them apart by the end of their ids, **Pixel 7 (0718)** and **Pixel 7 (3F2A)**, until you **Rename…** one.

The card shows up when a device with the same name and system pairs after the old one was last seen, for two weeks. Missed it? Open the old phone's page and **Forget** it.

## ⚙️ What you set up again

Settings that live on the phone itself stay with the old phone:

- 🔔 notification access, and which apps you mirror
- 💬 SMS and call permissions
- 🔓 Shizuku, root or LSPosed power-ups

Turn each on again on the new phone when you need it. Ferret asks in context, like the first time.

## 🧹 Old devices you never replaced

A device nobody has seen for 30 days shows up on Home as **1 not seen in 30 days · Remove…**. Tap it to clean up.

## 🆙 "Update Ferret on…"

Back after a while? One device may have updated Ferret and the other not. When the two no longer speak the same version, the device says so instead of staying offline:

- **Needs a newer Ferret**: update Ferret on that device, then open it once.
- **Update Ferret here to connect**: update this one. On a computer, **Get the update** opens the download page.

They connect again by themselves after the update. After an update, Home also shows one quiet line, **Updated to Ferret 0.2.0 · What's new**, once.
