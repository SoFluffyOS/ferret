# iPhone on Windows

> **TL;DR**
>
> 1. In Ferret: **Settings → Other apps → Connect an iPhone**.
> 2. In Windows: **Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Add device → Bluetooth**, pick the iPhone, confirm the code on both.
> 3. Back in Ferret, tap **Use** next to the iPhone.

You need Windows 10 (version 1703 or later) or Windows 11, and a Bluetooth adapter that does Bluetooth LE. Every recent laptop has one.

## 🔑 Pair the iPhone

1. Unlock the iPhone. Open **Settings → Bluetooth** and **keep that screen open**. The iPhone is only visible while it's showing.
2. In Ferret, open **Settings → Other apps** and turn on **Connect an iPhone**.
3. Tap **Bluetooth settings**. In Windows, choose **Add device → Bluetooth** and pick your iPhone.
4. Both screens show a code. Same code? Confirm on both.
5. Come back to Ferret. The iPhone is in the list. Tap **Use**.
6. The iPhone asks to share notifications with the PC, right after pairing or when Ferret first connects. **Allow** it.

Ferret connects and says **Connected to …**. Play a song on the iPhone to see the card.

## 📱 Check the iPhone's switch

On the iPhone, open **Settings → Bluetooth** and tap **ⓘ** next to your PC. **Share System Notifications** must be on. If Ferret says the iPhone isn't sharing, this is the switch.

## 🧭 Good to know

- Phone Link can use the same iPhone. Only one app gets the notifications at a time, so close Phone Link's iPhone link if Ferret stays silent.
- The iPhone doesn't show up in Ferret's list? Tap **Look again**. Still nothing? Remove the iPhone from Windows, tap **Forget This Device** on the iPhone, and pair again with the iPhone's Bluetooth screen open.
- After the PC sleeps, Ferret reconnects by itself within half a minute.

This part of Ferret was written against Microsoft's and Apple's documentation and still needs testing on more PCs. If it doesn't connect on yours, tell us the Windows version and the Bluetooth adapter.
