# Ferret

**F.E.R.R.E.T.** · **F**ast **E**ncrypted **R**elay for **R**esuming **E**very **T**ask

Start something on your phone. Pick it up on your laptop. And back again.

Ferret carries what you're doing between your own devices: pages, media, your clipboard, files, notifications, messages and calls. It works on Android, iOS, macOS, Linux and Windows.

- 🔒 **Local network only.** Devices talk directly, end-to-end encrypted.
- 🙅 **No account, no cloud, no telemetry.**
- 🔓 **Open source.** GPL-3.0 apps and an MIT protocol anyone can implement.

## 🚀 Get started

> **TL;DR**
>
> 1. Install Ferret on two of your devices.
> 2. Pair them once. Both screens show the same six digits.
> 3. Copy on one, paste on the other. That's it.

**1. Install it.** Pick your device, the guide has the download:

- 🍎 [Mac](getting-started/mac.md): a menubar app, macOS 12 or later.
- 🪟 [Windows](getting-started/windows.md): installs just for you, no admin.
- 🐧 [Linux](getting-started/linux.md): Flatpak, AppImage or .deb.
- 🤖 [Android](getting-started/android.md): the APK from GitHub.
- 📱 [iPhone](getting-started/iphone.md): not in the first release yet. It builds from source today.

**2. Pair your devices.** Open Ferret on both, on the same Wi-Fi, and tap **Pair**. See [Pair your devices](getting-started/pairing.md).

**3. Pick what you need.** Everything is off until you turn it on:

- 📋 [Clipboard](features/clipboard.md): copy here, paste there.
- 📦 [Drop](features/drop.md): files and folders, to any of your devices.
- 🔔 [Notifications](features/notifications.md), [messages](features/messages.md) and [calls](features/calls.md) from your Android phone.
- 🖥️ [Screen](features/mirroring.md): your phone's screen in a window.

Every other feature has its page in the sidebar.

Used another phone app for years? [Coming from another app](getting-started/coming-from-another-app.md) shows where each feature you know lives in Ferret.

## 🔒 Your data

Your devices talk straight to each other. No server sits in the middle. The [privacy page](privacy.md) lists every byte that goes anywhere else.
